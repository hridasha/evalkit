# Retail Stores

Northwind has 3 retail stores, in Portland, Denver and Burlington. Stores are open from 10 AM to 7 PM Monday to Saturday, and from 11 AM to 5 PM on Sunday.

Any store accepts returns when you bring the receipt. Online orders can be picked up in store for free, and they are ready within 2 hours. The Portland flagship store runs a gear repair workshop every Saturday.
