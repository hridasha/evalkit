# Privacy

We collect your name, email address, shipping address and order history. We never sell personal data to anyone.

Order records are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it. You can ask us to delete your data by emailing privacy@northwind.example, and we finish the deletion within 30 days.

Payment card numbers are handled by our payment partner and are never stored on our own servers. We use cookies only to remember the contents of your shopping cart.
