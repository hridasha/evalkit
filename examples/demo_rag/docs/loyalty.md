# Trail Club Loyalty Program

Joining the Trail Club is free. Members earn 1 point for every dollar spent, and 100 points can be exchanged for a $5 reward. Members also get a birthday bonus of 50 points.

Points expire after 18 months without any purchase. Members who reach 2000 points in a year become Gold members and get free express shipping on every order.

Trail Club members can shop sales 24 hours before everyone else.
