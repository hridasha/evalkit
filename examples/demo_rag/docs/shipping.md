# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $4.99. It is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.99. Overnight shipping costs $29.99 and the order must be placed before 2 PM Eastern.

We ship to the United States and Canada only. We do not ship to PO boxes with express or overnight service. Orders leave our warehouse within 1 business day, and a tracking number is emailed as soon as the parcel ships.

If a package is lost in transit, we replace it for free when it is reported within 30 days of the ship date.
