# Warranty

Every Northwind product has a 2 year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Tents and sleeping bags have a longer warranty of 5 years.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse, or damage from accidents. It is not transferable to a second owner.

To make a claim, email warranty@northwind.example with photos of the problem and your order number. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 10 business days.
