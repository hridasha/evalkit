# Refund Policy

Customers can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

Sale items can be returned within 14 days, but only for store credit. Items marked Final Sale cannot be returned at all.

Return shipping is free for orders over $50. For smaller orders a $6.95 return fee is taken from the refund. Please include the order number printed on the packing slip inside the parcel.
